# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of annual leave per year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month and can be taken once it has accrued. Requests are submitted in the HR portal and the manager approves them within 2 working days.

## Carry forward
Unused leave can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they are not used.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver gets 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.
