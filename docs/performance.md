# Performance and Growth

## Review cycle
Performance reviews are held twice a year: a mid-year review in April and an annual review in October. Ratings use a 5-point scale and include peer feedback from at least 3 colleagues.

## Promotions
Promotions are decided only in the annual cycle in October.

## Learning budget
Every employee has a learning budget of ₹50,000 per year for courses and certifications. Exam fees are reimbursed after the employee passes the exam and submits the certificate.
