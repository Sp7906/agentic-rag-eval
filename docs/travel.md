# Travel Policy

## Booking
Flights must be booked through the Travel Desk at least 14 days in advance. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and needs VP approval.

## Hotels and meals
The hotel limit is ₹8,000 per night in metro cities and ₹5,000 per night elsewhere. The meal allowance while travelling is ₹1,500 per day.

## International travel
Visa support requests must be raised at least 30 days before international travel.
