# Expense Reimbursement

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the Expense app within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected. Receipts are mandatory for any expense above ₹500.

## Approvals
Claims up to ₹25,000 are approved by the direct manager. Claims above ₹25,000 need approval from a director.

## Payment
Approved claims are reimbursed to the employee's bank account within 7 working days.
