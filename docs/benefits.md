# Employee Benefits

## Health insurance
Northwind provides health insurance of ₹5,00,000 per year for the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

## Provident fund
Northwind contributes 12% of basic pay to the provident fund. Employees can choose to contribute an additional amount voluntarily.

## Wellness allowance
Every employee gets a wellness allowance of ₹6,000 per year for gym memberships or fitness classes. Claims are submitted through the Expense app.

## Employee referral bonus
Employees who refer a successful hire receive a referral bonus of ₹30,000 after the new hire completes 3 months.
