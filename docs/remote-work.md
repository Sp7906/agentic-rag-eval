# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Northwind follows a hybrid model. Employees are required to be in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote days.

## Work from anywhere
Employees can work from another city or country for up to 20 days per year with manager approval. The location must be in a country where Northwind has a registered entity.

## Home office support
New remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of ₹20,000 for a desk and chair. Northwind also reimburses internet costs up to ₹1,000 per month.
