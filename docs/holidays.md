# Holidays and Office Hours

## Public holidays
Northwind observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday list is published every December on the intranet.

## Office hours
Core office hours are 10 am to 4 pm. Employees can start between 8 am and 10 am and finish accordingly.

## Floating holidays
Each employee gets 2 floating holidays per year to use for festivals of their choice. Floating holidays must be requested at least 7 days in advance.

## On-call allowance
Engineers on the on-call rota receive ₹2,000 per week of on-call duty.
