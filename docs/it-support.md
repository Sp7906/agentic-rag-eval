# IT Support

## Helpdesk
The IT helpdesk is open from 8 am to 8 pm IST on weekdays. Tickets are raised through the helpdesk portal.

## Response times
Critical (P1) tickets get a response within 1 hour. High (P2) tickets get a response within 4 hours. Other tickets are answered within 2 working days.

## Devices and software
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Software requests need manager approval and take 5 working days. Personal devices must not be used to access production systems.
